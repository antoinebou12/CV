# Real project assets only

Use real project screenshots, photographs, diagrams, or recorded output for article covers. Do not generate conceptual substitutes.

AINewsTracker, MistubaVideo, and Snapchat Lens Creator currently use `cover_none: true`. Their article pages, list/search entries, and social metadata omit cover imagery until a suitable real asset is available.

IPC Toolkit retains the existing repository architecture image. Other real project assets remain unchanged.

The rejected generated illustrations and their resized variants were moved out of the site source and publish output into a recoverable local backup under `tmp/rejected-generated-covers-20261027/`.
