## What the role covers

The LinkedIn post only names the title and the school. The CV entry (September 2024 to January 2026) describes the work itself:

- Built and maintained the GitLab workspace for the course: repositories, CI/CD templates, runners and workflows for approximately 20 concurrent teams.
- Coached Docker and Kubernetes build, deploy and debug sessions, with lab scenarios driven by OWASP guidance.
- Refreshed the DevSecOps lab artifacts, graded practicals and coached resubmissions.

## Why it mattered to me

Running a GitLab workspace for twenty teams at once is a small platform job: templates have to be reusable, runners have to stay healthy during deadline weeks, and every lab has to be reproducible on a student laptop. It is the same discipline I apply to my own CI/CD and homelab work, which is why this role sits next to the platform engineering experience on the CV.
