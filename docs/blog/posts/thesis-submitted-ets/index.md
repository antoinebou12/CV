- Author: Antoine Boucher (https://antoineboucher.info/CV/index-en.html)I submitted my master's thesis at ÉTS. The work is real-time simulation of surface wear, at the meeting point of physical simulation, contact, and graphics rendering.

Sheldon Andrews supervised the thesis. Paul Kry, Yinchu Liu, Loïc Nassif, Nicolas Rosset, Quoc-Minh Ton-That, Alexandre Mercier-Aubin, and Mélissa Katz gave advice and help through the project.

The technical write-up on wear in texture space is coming soon. The [thesis article](https://antoineboucher.info/CV/blog/posts/thesis-wear-texture-space/) has the current project overview.
