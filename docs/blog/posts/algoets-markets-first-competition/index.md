- Author: Antoine Boucher (https://antoineboucher.info/CV/index-en.html)While I was president of [AlgoÉTS](https://github.com/AlgoETS), the club ran its first campus-wide paper-trading competition on **MarkÉTS**. The numbers below come from the club summary featured on my LinkedIn profile.

![Audience during an AlgoÉTS event](https://antoineboucher.info/CV/blog/posts/algoets-markets-first-competition/images/evening-audience.png)

Over about four months:

- **4,154** orders executed
- **170+** participants
- Leaderboards with returns near **60%**
- Platform uptime above **99%**
- **15+** features shipped during the live competition
- **6** new developers, club size **20** members

MarkÉTS was built and maintained by students. Partners included ÉTS and FÉRIQUE. The next steps named in that summary were platform work, ML and AI strategies, and more contests.
