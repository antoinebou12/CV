While I was president of [AlgoÉTS](https://github.com/AlgoETS), the club ran its first campus-wide paper-trading competition on **MarkÉTS**. The numbers below come from the club summary featured on my LinkedIn profile. **[Version française]({{< ref "/posts/algoets-markets-first-competition/index.fr.md" >}})**.

<!--more-->

{{< figure src="images/evening-audience.png" alt="Audience during an AlgoÉTS event" caption="Campus energy around AlgoÉTS events." loading="lazy" >}}


Over about four months:

- **4,154** orders executed
- **170+** participants
- Leaderboards with returns near **60%**
- Platform uptime above **99%**
- **15+** features shipped during the live competition
- **6** new developers, club size **20** members

MarkÉTS was built and maintained by students. Partners included ÉTS and FÉRIQUE. The next steps named in that summary were platform work, ML and AI strategies, and more contests.
