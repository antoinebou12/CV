Excited to share that few years, I've been creating Snapchat lenses for both myself and clients on Fiverr. 

I'm proud to say that I've created a total of 42 lenses between 2017 and 2020!

While some lenses rejected by Snapchat and clients, I used these experiences as opportunities to improve my skills. 

I even developed my own script to convert GIF/Video to PNG sequence, which has since been used by many people who are also creating Snapchat lenses. (https://lnkd.in/gN-DFZX7)

It's been a rewarding experience to turn my interest in creating AR filters into a side hustle, and I'm excited to continue exploring new opportunities in this field. In fact, I even had the chance to talk with Snapchat over the phone to provide feedback on Lens Studio and help make it a better tool for creators.

I'm also interested in exploring new tools and platforms for creating AR effects. 

If you know of any good tutorials for Meta Spark that would be helpful for Lens Studio users, I'd love to hear your recommendations!
