I spent two days at **Expo Manger Santé 2023** in **Place des Congrès**, Montreal — part work on an **olive kiosk**, part wandering the aisles for things I would actually buy again. Event photos by **OS7Media** (os7mediamatrix@gmail.com); thanks for letting me use a few here. **[Version française]({{< ref "/posts/expo-manger-sante-2023/index.fr.md" >}})**.

## Olive kiosk

I like olives — taste and the usual “actually good for you” story — and two days of samples and conversation turned into about **$300** in sales. The number is small; the useful part was talking with people who cared about the product, not just walking past a booth.

## Food worth tasting

The expo is loud and generous with samples. **Gen V** lettuces were the produce highlight: crisp enough that you notice after a day of crackers and dips.

![Fresh produce at the expo](./images/genV.jpg)

## Booths that stood out

**BKind** looked polished and priced like it — interesting if you track natural cosmetics, not my cart that weekend.

![BKind display](./images/bkind-products.jpg)

Next door, **Mate Libre** made the neighbour kiosk the best part of the row. I drink maté anyway; their **matéina** line was the find.

![Mate Libre kiosk](./images/matelibre.jpeg)

**Wise** smoothie mixes (green and red) are going into my rushed-morning rotation — add liquid, blend, leave.

![Wise smoothie mixes](./images/wise-smoothies.jpg)

## Afterward

Expo Manger Santé is part trade show, part tasting menu. Glad I went, and I would do another edition if the timing works.
