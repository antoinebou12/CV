I presented a poster at CAÉC 2026, the annual congress of student researchers, on real-time simulation of visual texture wear fed by experimental data. I placed second in the Optics category.

<!--more-->

The conversations around the poster covered automatic commit classification, training models with other models, and optics. I also talked with Samuel Lefebvre about virtual-reality interaction and how to make virtual worlds more credible. Leyla Agosti Cagalli Burri hosted the closing ceremony. The LinkedIn post links a paper related to work presented at the congress.

The thesis write-up is [the article on wear in texture space]({{< ref "/posts/thesis-wear-texture-space/index.md" >}}).
