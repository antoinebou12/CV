**GraphQuon** (formerly MOTOGRAPH) is the annual Quebec–Ontario pre-SIGGRAPH workshop for East-Canadian computer graphics labs. The **2024** edition ran **9–10 November 2024** at **École de technologie supérieure (ÉTS)** in Montréal.

Official site: [graphquon.github.io](https://graphquon.github.io/).
