Who is Antoine Boucher?

Antoine Boucher is a software engineer in Montreal whose work spans platform engineering, backend systems, Kubernetes, GitLab CI/CD, Terraform, and interactive graphics with C++/OpenGL and Blender. His public CV, blog, and open-source projects are published at antoineboucher.info.

Platform engineering means the services, CI/CD pipelines, containers, cloud infrastructure, and observability that help product teams ship reliably. Graphics refers to real-time rendering, shaders, simulation, and Blender tooling as a technical research track, not a pure artist portfolio.

What is his experience with secure CI/CD and Kubernetes?

What is uml-mcp?

uml-mcp is an open-source Model Context Protocol server that turns architecture prompts into UML and related diagrams using Mermaid, PlantUML, and Kroki. Repository: github.com/antoinebou12/uml-mcp
