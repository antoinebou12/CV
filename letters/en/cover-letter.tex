I am a software engineer working toward a master's in information technology engineering at ÉTS. Lately that has meant platforms and backends (Kubernetes, Terraform, GitLab CI/CD, GCP, Java, Kotlin, Python) with DevSecOps mixed in from research, industry, and teaching. I also keep a research thread in computer graphics (OpenGL, C++, Blender). The PDF résumé and \href{https://antoineboucher.info}{antoineboucher.info} spell out the rest.

At IMC2---the Institut multidisciplinaire en cybersécurité et cyberrésilience---I was a part-time research assistant through early 2026. I kept GitLab CI/CD and runners working for services on GCP, used Terraform for rollouts, ran containerized workloads, and tightened Java microservices with automated tests while lining Android builds up with the same promotion path. I contributed to analyst-facing tooling that scores and triages cybersecurity risks at scale. It was a useful mix of cluster plumbing, tests that catch real failures, and a UI people use under time pressure.

I have also been a teaching assistant for LOG8100 DevSecOps at Polytechnique Montréal and for several software-heavy courses at ÉTS: shared GitLab spaces for many concurrent teams, CI templates, runners, Azure-backed labs, and steady coaching on Docker, Kubernetes, databases, backends, and secure delivery when deadlines are fixed. I default to naming the next concrete step after I explain the idea.

Earlier roles include cloud work at IONODES, Kotlin and Spring services at Intact, and contract full-stack work with Django, Vue, observability tooling, and APIs. I picked up production habits I still lean on: narrow logs, dashboards that answer real questions, migrations you can back out of, and scope conversations that do not end in a surprise outage.

Outside that cadence I work on interactive graphics and surface-wear simulation. In public I maintain \href{https://github.com/antoinebou12/uml-mcp}{\texttt{uml-mcp}}, MCP-oriented diagram tooling, because assistants behave more predictably when the host exposes clear tool contracts.

I am looking for a team that writes decisions down, uses review as a teaching loop more than a gate, and bakes security and quality into delivery instead of bolting them on at the end. Thank you for reading; I would welcome a conversation about how this background fits your opening.

\end{spacing}
